# Awwwards 2026 Winners and Nominees — Mechanics Sweep

Fourteen 2026 Awwwards case pages read at component level yield thirteen mechanics beyond the parent reference's catalog, three of the fourteen adding none. Two of the dependencies those mechanics need are absent from the stack the rest of the corpus runs, Rive and Curtains.js, and Web Audio as a scroll- or chapter-driven narrative layer is otherwise cataloged only in the [contenders sweep](./site-of-the-year-contenders.md) (Cartier, Omega). One of the fourteen sits outside the 2026 cycle: MindMarket took Site of the Day (SOTD) on 29 Dec 2025 and Site of the Month (SOTM) Dec 2025, and is carried here for its Rive path-scroll. Archetype labels follow the taxonomy of the parent reference: [Award-Winning Websites — 2025–2030 Reference](../award-winning-websites-2025-2030.md).

Reliability legend: **[TAG]** = from Awwwards technology tags · **[LABEL]** = from Awwwards curated interaction-element labels · **[DESC]** = author description quote · `(inferred)` = an implementation detail extrapolated rather than stated · `(stated, unconfirmed)` = a claim the case page does not confirm. SOTM months are read from the [monthly listing](https://www.awwwards.com/websites/sites_of_the_month/). Case pages read 16 Jul 2026, the listing 29 Aug 2026.

## What the sweep adds

- **Highest-novelty, best-evidenced new mechanics** (all from reliable Awwwards LABELs/TAGs, not inference): #3 3D→2D→3D morph (Oryzo), #4 cursor-particle footer (Oryzo), #5 Curtains.js plane distortion (Indigo), #8 playable WebGL game (Lacoste), #9 Rive path-scroll (MindMarket), #10 sticky stacked cards (MindMarket).
- **Two external dependencies** beyond the GSAP / Three.js / Lenis stack the rest of the corpus runs on: **Rive** (`@rive-app/canvas`) and **Curtains.js**. Both are small and drop-in.
- **Web Audio (#6)** as a narrative layer is otherwise cataloged only in the contenders sweep (Cartier, Omega); the sound gates on Bruno Simon, Siena, and unseen.co are entry toggles, not scored layers. Cheapest to add (vanilla), high perceived-craft payoff on immersive builds.
- **Award months.** Floema, Oryzo, GQ&AP, Renaissance, and MindMarket each hold a SOTD on their case page and a Site of the Month on the listing. Lacoste and SÉVE read as **Nominee** on their case pages, not SOTD.
